Takeaways used in v2:
1. The frame means one thing: the level. Profile completion and verification stay out of it.
2. Level = colour + structure + the written word; never colour alone (gold on white is 1.86:1).
3. Material comes from engraving (double lines, micro-text, a star), never from gradients, glow or shine.
4. Arc text is Latin capitals and digits only; the Arabic level name sits on a straight line beside the avatar.
5. Motion only when the level changes: the ring draws once (600 ms), nothing loops.
